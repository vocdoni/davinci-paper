It is responsible for the life-cycle management of voting processes. It includes the initiation, monitoring, execution, and closure of voting events.

\subsubsection{Sequencer registry} 

It keeps track of the existing available sequencers, stores the collateral of the sequencers to ensure (that incentivizes) good behaviour, and is used to coordinate the distributed key generation when a new voting process is created.

\subsubsection{Results verification}

For each voting process, this smart contract maintains the integrity of the cast votes and process life cycle. It verifies that each state transition committed by a sequencer adheres to the predefined rules.
