\begin{abstract}
	\Davinci is the evolution of the Vocdoni voting protocol, designed to empower civil society by providing essential tools for secure, verifiable, and ballot-private digital voting. Using recent advancements in \ac{ZK} proof systems and blockchain technology, \davinci operates as a specialized \ac{ZK} rollup that inherits network security from its settlement layer, either the Ethereum mainnet or an Ethereum rollup built on the OP Stack. Voters prove that their encrypted ballots are well-formed with a \ac{ZK-SNARK}, and sequencers prove each batch of ballots inside a \ac{zkVM}: a single succinct proof attests to the verification of every ballot proof, signature, and census proof in the batch, to the re-encryption of the ballots, and to the resulting update of the election state and of the encrypted tally. The system relies on cryptographic proofs to ensure integrity and security, reducing the trust placed in the operators that process ballots and tally the results. By integrating \acsp{ZK-SNARK}, a \ac{zkVM}, and threshold homomorphic encryption, \davinci enables individual and universal verifiability, ballot privacy, and reduced trust in election processes. The protocol distributes the election key among a set of key wardens through distributed key generation, coordinates the process through a single registry smart contract, and publishes in Ethereum data blobs enough data for anyone to rebuild the election state. Voters may revote, and the silent re-randomization of stored ballots obscures revotes from outside observers, which mitigates vote buying and coercion without a trusted tally server. With a focus on accessibility, scalability, coercion mitigation, and automation, \davinci aims to facilitate high-frequency and low-cost voting, fostering mass adoption of voting and simplifying civil participation.
	%
	Finally, the design of \davinci is grounded in practicality: all components are
	implemented using current technologies and evaluated on production-grade hardware. In sum, the proposed architecture is not merely theoretical, but a working system. The source code has been made open source, allowing practitioners and researchers to further investigate its details and potential for future uses.
\end{abstract}
